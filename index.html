The shop

Oakmont started with one chair, one clipper, and a simple idea: give people the haircut they actually asked for.

We are a booking-first shop. No queue by the door, no rushing you out of the chair — just two barbers who take the time to get it right, every visit. Fresh towels, a good coffee, and a room that feels like somewhere you would want to sit for an hour.

Six years in, most of our clients have been coming since the beginning. We would like to think that says enough.
